Under the hood

A vertical slice with real engineering behind it.

  • 20/20 automated checks — movement, combos, i-frames, cones, LOS, parry, unblockables, hit-stop, boss phases — green on every build.
  • Light budget 28 — Godot's Compatibility renderer silently drops lights past 32, so every light in the level is budgeted and verified.
  • Rebuildable — the whole level is generated from code; CC0 assets are fetched by script. Clone, run, rebuild.
  • Deterministic retarget — UAL2's 43 clips share the character's skeleton, so retargeting is a track-path rewrite, not a bake.
